# Roost Family — Design Update 2: Routine Icon Artwork

Paste this into Claude Design (same project as "Roost Family App v2").

## The ask

Turn the 12 routine icon "keys" in the v2 Routine icon library panel into **final illustrated artwork**. The rest of v2 is approved and going to implementation; this is the only open design deliverable.

## Who reads these icons

Pre-readers aged about 2–7, glancing at a picture schedule on a kitchen tablet. They must recognize each icon **without the label**, from arm's length, in about one second.

## Icon set

The 12 existing keys, plus 12 more so a typical day can be built without photos:

- **Existing keys:** breakfast, teeth, shoes, car, nap, bath, books, bed, potty, park, snack, getting dressed
- **Add:** lunch, dinner, wash hands, jacket, daycare/school, playtime, TV/screen time, tidy up, music, grandparents' visit, doctor, swimming

## Requirements

- **Style:** friendly and simple, matching the warm v2 palette and the rounded line style of the Gable logo. Filled shapes are fine (and probably better for toddlers) as long as the set is consistent.
- **Sizes:** must read at **120 pt** (current step card) and **64 pt** (upcoming steps in the strip).
- **Formats:** SVG, one icon per file, 1:1 artboard, no text inside the icon.
- **Color:** works on the Kids' Corner background `#F5E6C4` and the tile color `#FBF3E0`. No meaning carried by color alone.
- **No people with specific skin tones or genders.** Use objects, or neutral, simple figures.
- **Photo replacement state:** show one step card where a parent's photo replaces the icon (rounded crop, same card frame), so implementation knows how the two coexist.
- **Completed state:** show the checked/done look for a step card.

## Deliver

- A panel with all 24 icons at 120 pt and 64 pt
- The photo-replacement and completed step-card states
- The SVGs exported in the handoff bundle
